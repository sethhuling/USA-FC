Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Draft — this policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be finalized before any public, monetized launch.

Who we are

Uncle Sam FC is an independent fan app that tracks American soccer players at clubs around the world. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Soccer or any club, league, or publisher. Contact: UncleSamFCapp@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

The app works without an account. Depending on what you use, we store:

  • An anonymous device identifier — a random ID created on your device. It ties your favorites and notification settings to your device and is not linked to your name unless you sign in.
  • Your favorites — the players you star.
  • A push subscription — only if you turn notifications on: the delivery address your browser's push service assigns, used solely to send the notifications you asked for.
  • Account basics, if you choose to sign in — your email address (magic-link sign-in) or your Google account's basic profile (name, email) via Google sign-in. Used only to sync favorites across your devices. We never see your Google password.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or any advertising-tracking identifiers.

Where it lives

This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States, and is transmitted over HTTPS. Match and player statistics shown in the app come from a licensed sports-data API and involve no personal data.

What we don't do

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers named above (Supabase for storage, Google if you use Google sign-in) and your browser's push service for delivering notifications.

Cookies, local storage, and advertising

The app uses your browser's local storage for functional preferences (your favorites, settings, and sign-in session). It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Uncle Sam FC currently shows no ads. If advertising is added in the future, this section will be updated to describe the ad provider, its cookies, your consent choices (including a consent tool for visitors in the EU/UK and rights under US state privacy laws), and the effective date above will change.

Deleting your data

  • Turning notifications off in the My Club tab removes your push subscription.
  • Un-starring players removes those favorites.
  • To delete your account or everything tied to your device, email UncleSamFCapp@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Uncle Sam FC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date at the top.
